From the user story and the Jira task list I wrote for the program.

  • A gap analysis against SaaS affiliate programs such as HubSpot and ClickUp, covering commission structure, tools and resources for affiliates, accessibility and compliance.
  • A comparison of nine affiliate management tools on best fit, pricing, integrations, tracking, multi-currency and fraud detection.
  • A user story for the affiliate, with acceptance criteria for registration, marketing materials, a unique link and promo code, and tracking and analytics.
  • A Jira task list in ten areas: registration, dashboard, marketing materials, performance tracking, support, promotion, landing page, affiliate link, content and release notes.
  • The affiliate agreement in four drafts and the cookie policy in two versions.
  • A presentation outline for stakeholders and the CEO: purpose, benefits, how it works, tracking and support.
  • Release note 1.0, in English and Arabic, dated 23 July 2024.

Decisions

Study the market before buildingThe gap analysis found where other programs were stronger, for example recurring commissions and more precise tracking, and recommended actions for each.
One promo code next to the linkIn the June 2024 dashboard review I proposed a promo code card, with the code placed next to the affiliate link so it is easy to copy, and the code issued as soon as the affiliate is approved.
Remove what duplicatesThree dashboard cards repeated what the commission page already showed, so I proposed removing them. I also flagged a portal logo instead of the Vconnct logo, an Arabic switch that did not change the page, and links that must stay active and tied to the landing page and website.
Make the rules explicitAffiliates accept the agreement, privacy policy and terms at registration. The cookie policy sets a 90-day window and covers retargeting.
Give affiliates a communityMarketing materials sit in a Telegram channel.

How it was tracked

  • Each affiliate had their own promo code, tracked in a sheet.
  • Orders and affiliate payments were tracked by month in separate sheets.
