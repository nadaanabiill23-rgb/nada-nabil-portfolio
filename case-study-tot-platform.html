Overview

TOT Platform is a company with many products under it. I worked on the foundation of all the products below as the Product Manager: discovery, analysis, requirements, backlogs and documentation, and for My Guest, planning and following delivery.

For the products other than My Guest, I worked on the foundation (the base documentation). I do not know if they were published.

My Guest (hotel services app)

  • A client project: an app where hotel guests request services, with an admin side to manage requests, facilities and content.
  • Web and application design for the app.
  • Worked on the backlog, features and prioritization, and an enhancements list (Feb to Mar 2026).
  • Planned the work after UI/UX approval: an 8-week plan covering planning and scope freeze, authentication and stay verification, VAT and billing, home and CMS, facility and service structure, multi-guest and cancellation, then testing and final approval.
  • Examples of the planned scope: Google login, session handling with auto logout at checkout, VAT as a separate field and in invoices, events and news content, multi-guest roles, cancellation rules.

While I was there, the MVP was delivered to the client. The team then started redesigning the whole design and developing the existing features to fit the requirements. I left the company and handed it over to someone to continue.

Parking management system

RoleProduct Manager
Launch statusI worked on the product foundation. I do not know its launch status.
  • A smart parking operations platform: vehicle entry to exit, payment, monitoring and reporting. Gate control with tickets, QR or plate recognition, and digital tickets.
  • Wrote the discovery pack (dated 30 Dec 2025): business overview, who it serves, problems solved, and the solution.
  • Analysis workbook: problems, bottlenecks, opportunities, competitive gaps and scope.
  • Product backlog pack with modules grouped into platform core (users, roles, organizations, locations, zones, settings, audit logs, notifications) and access and gate control (gates, access methods, vehicle registry), plus an access management workbook.

E-commerce platform

RoleProduct Manager
Launch statusI worked on the product foundation. I do not know its launch status.
  • A modular e-commerce platform: customizable online store, a unified CMS dashboard and analytics, built to connect with TOT modules such as PIM, OMS, CRM, POS and last-mile delivery. Aimed at retailers and businesses in Egypt and the Middle East, with Arabic and RTL support.
  • Wrote the discovery pack (dated 26 Nov 2025) and the analysis workbook.
  • Built a large product backlog pack and a requirements pack.
  • Arranged the modules into product domains with an execution order that starts with platform foundation, identity and governance, so later modules can depend on it.
  • Prepared two sales enablement presentations (drafts).
  • Designed shopping interfaces and product browsing experiences for ElKhabaz and Elbda3a.

Goods.eg (construction classifieds, Arabic)

RoleProduct Manager
Launch statusI worked on the product foundation. I do not know its launch status.
  • Evaluated the product strategy: the platform mixed a classified ads marketplace, a factory and supplier directory, and construction service listings in one structure.
  • Found the core issue: unclear product identity, fragmented user journeys, weak trust and no clear revenue mechanism.
  • Wrote an enhancements scope in Arabic that splits the platform into four sections (products, service providers, real estate, factories and suppliers).

HR platform (Enterprise HR / Workforce OS)

RoleProduct Manager

  • A unified HR platform to replace spreadsheets: recruitment, core HR, attendance, payroll and performance.
  • Wrote the discovery pack and analysis, and a large feature workbook.
  • Mapped an Enterprise Workforce OS feature tree across 15 areas, from platform foundation, workforce lifecycle and payroll to performance, analytics and an AI assistant, with a status mark per feature.

Fleet management and asset management

RoleProduct Manager
Launch statusI worked on the product foundation. I do not know its launch status.
  • Fleet management: a platform for vehicles, drivers, trips, fuel and costs. Discovery pack (26 Nov 2025), analysis, requirements, and a feature workbook with user stories across vehicles, trips, drivers, maintenance, fuel and energy, and dashboards and reporting.
  • Asset management: a system for small and medium businesses to track physical and digital assets across their lifecycle. Discovery pack (30 Dec 2025), analysis and requirements.

The way we documented products

  • Each product followed the same eight-part documentation set: discovery, analysis, UX research and design, business modeling, product architecture, requirements and backlog, development (SDLC) and release notes.
  • I made all the documentation templates myself. The whole team then adopted them, so everyone followed one single way of documenting.
