Why complex dashboards fail when information hierarchy is weak.

Dashboards do not fail because they contain too much data. They fail when users cannot tell what matters first.

Situation

Many SaaS dashboards try to show everything at once: charts, tables, statuses, alerts, shortcuts, and filters.

Problem

When everything has the same visual weight, users must spend cognitive effort deciding what to inspect.

Product Decision

Design hierarchy around the user's main decision: monitor, compare, investigate, or act.

Metric To Watch

Task completion time, feature adoption, support tickets, repeated filter use, and return frequency.
