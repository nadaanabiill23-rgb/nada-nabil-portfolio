Case Study 03 | Rosy Calc

Redesigning every flow of a period tracking app, from documentation to the store.

Rosy Calc is a period and menstrual cycle tracking app. I was the Product Manager: I redesigned the existing flows, wrote the tasks and user flows, and followed design and development until the app went live on Google Play and the App Store in November 2026.

Role: Product Manager / UX DesignerWomen's health, period trackingTara Digital
Challenge

The app recorded cycle data but gave users little back. Cycle tracking was not clearly the core of the product.

Approach

Documented the product, found the gaps, redesigned all existing flows, and wrote new flows and backlog items for the designer and developers.

Scope

18 flows for the existing cycle logic, 30 new flows in 9 areas, a backlog with user stories and acceptance criteria.

Product and gaps

  • Documented the product: overview, current features, information architecture, data requirements and feature specifications.
  • Rosy Calc covers cycle tracking, period, ovulation and fertile window predictions, symptom and health tracking, notifications, community, and profile.
  • Found gaps in the current product: unclear positioning, no hierarchy between core and supporting features, collected data used only for recording, no strong cycle history, and predictions based on basic information.
  • Direction I set: cycle tracking is the core, with symptoms, insights and predictions around it, and community as support.

Redesigning the flows

  • Redesigned all existing flows and followed the work with the designer.
  • Wrote 18 user flows for the existing cycle logic, for example first-time setup, log, edit and delete a period, irregular cycles, missed or delayed period, overlapping periods, and insufficient data.
  • Wrote the cycle tracking and prediction logic, and the notification cases.

New flows

30 new user flows across 9 areas:

  • Cycle history and statistics
  • Daily health check-in
  • Symptom tracking
  • Personalized insights
  • Cycle summary
  • Smart notifications
  • Privacy and data
  • Cycle education
  • Smart cycle assistant

Each area has its own flows, plus a feature backlog with epics, user stories and acceptance criteria. All 30 new flows were implemented in the live version.

Delivery to the store

  • Followed development and design until the app went live on Google Play and the App Store in November 2026.
  • Fixed the tasks and the content the team needed.
  • Made quick design changes myself when they did not need to wait for the designer.
