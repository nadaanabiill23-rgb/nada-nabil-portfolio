Feature decision breakdown

When to ship an MVP instead of a complete workflow.

A smaller release is useful when it tests a risky assumption, creates real user value, and avoids locking the team into the wrong direction.

Situation

Stakeholders request a complete workflow, but the team has limited evidence about which parts users need most.

Problem

Shipping the full workflow may increase delivery time and hide whether the core assumption is valid.

Product Decision

Ship the smallest useful version that validates behavior, then expand based on adoption and feedback.

Metric To Watch

Adoption rate, repeat usage, completion rate, qualitative feedback, and number of follow-up requests.
