Why the restructure started

The app already had a released version. The team is now rebuilding its structure, design and flows because:

  • The code was not well organized.
  • The design needed to change and be more interactive.
  • Many user flows were missing.
  • Interactions in the app were not smooth or well suited to the experience.

The direction for the learning experience: a faster question plan, questions ordered by student level and refreshed so students do not keep seeing the same ones, and a question bank where students can review their past answers, the corrections and the solutions to wrong answers.

Decision example: starting without a placement test

Should a new student have to finish the placement test before using the app? I specified that they should not. Students can skip it and keep practicing, and the app builds its picture of them from their answers.

1Student skipsPlacement status saved as Skipped. No final result is created.
2Practice startsDefault difficulty is Medium until there is enough data.
3Answers build dataEach skill stays "Insufficient Data" until 5 scored answers.
4Skill is classifiedWeak, Developing or Strong, skill by skill.
Rules I definedSkipping never blocks the app. Accuracy counts scored answers only, not unanswered questions. Each skill is classified on its own data. Weak is under 50%, Developing is 50% to under 80%, Strong is 80% or more.
Kept separate on purposeSkipping the test does not grant any subscription or access. Paid content still depends on the student's entitlement. The placement test status and the skill classification are two different states.
Actors and dependenciesStudent, mobile app, backend, adaptive engine, subscription service and entitlement service. The flow depends on sessions, student profile, performance tracking, training sessions and subscription management.
Return pathA student who skipped can come back and take the placement test later, unless they already completed it. A completed test cannot be retaken.

My part was the flow itself: the rules, the actors, the edge cases and the acceptance criteria that engineering builds against. The adaptive engine and the interface work are separate parts of the product.
