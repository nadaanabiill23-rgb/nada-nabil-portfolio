Product focus

  • Initial product scope and target audience.
  • Onboarding and profile setup requirements.
  • Pregnancy timeline and weekly tracking experience.
  • Week navigation and progress indicators.
  • Symptom tracking and warning symptoms.
  • Notification scenarios and milestone interactions.
  • Cultural and localization requirements for Saudi Arabia.
  • Pregnancy Calendar epic (month view, daily timeline, milestones, symptoms, reminders), written as a PRD and a designer brief.
  • Product backlog for new features: 27 features in 9 epics (health tracking, safety and education, planning, personalization, reminders, AI assistant, memories, wellbeing, future expansion) with priorities.
  • 77 new and enhancement user flows, from daily check-in to birth plan.
  • Competitive benchmark against Flo, Ovia, Pregnancy+ and What to Expect, turned into product opportunities.
  • Baby & Mom Store feature definition for the Saudi market, with 13 user flows. Specified only, design and development not started. Planned for a later step.
  • UI/UX task list for comment improvements (composer, attachments, replies, quotes).
