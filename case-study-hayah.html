Case Study 02 | Hayah

Turning a complex community app into a structured, documented product.

Hayah is a community mobile app where users discover groups, publish content, interact with communities and engage with other users. I worked on it from the start: research, defining the features and user flows, then working with the team from design to development.

Product focus

I assessed the app's features, structured it into modules and epics, and documented requirements, rules and flows.

  • Research and product feature analysis.
  • Product vision: an Arabic social community platform built around interest-based groups, meaningful content, trust and safety. Primary audience: women looking for trusted communities, including mothers and pregnant women.
  • Six personas: group member, content creator, group moderator, group owner, platform administrator, and a healthcare expert (future).
  • Defined the features and user flows.
  • Worked with the team from design to development. Design is finished and implementation is in progress.
  • Modules and epics.
  • Functional requirements and business rules.
  • User roles, permissions and moderation requirements.
  • Content creation and community management experiences.
  • Edge cases and alternative scenarios.

Documented modules

Authentication and onboarding, home feed, groups and communities, categories, search and discovery, content creation, posts and media, comments, notifications, user profiles, messaging, moderation and safety, gamification, AI features, analytics and admin.

MVP and phased roadmap

  • Defined the MVP around one core loop: sign up, onboarding and interests, home, discover and join groups, read posts, interact, create a post, search, profile, notifications and messaging.
  • Rule for the MVP: every feature that goes in works end to end. Basic safety, moderation and admin go in from the first release, because the app is built on user-generated content.
  • AI, advanced personalization and advanced gamification are deferred until there is real usage and data.
  • Phased roadmap by module: MVP, then three later phases (discovery and engagement, personalization and gamification, then AI and advanced features).

Where it stands

Design is finished and the team is working on implementation. The app has not launched yet.
